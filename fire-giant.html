The Fire Giant

Overview

In an age vastly preceding the existence of the Golden Lineage, the Giants occupied the frigid region of the North, worshipping the Fell God of Flame to attain close to godlike power . The giants created the Great Forge, a gigantic crucible in order to tend and protect the all-powerful flame of the Fell God. Also inhabiting the region were the Zamor Knights, who became the arch nemeses to the Giants, and would eventually be an aid to their downfall.

Tragedy of the Giants

When the Age of the Erdtree and the Golden Lineage was formed by Queen Marika, she quickly commanded her consort Godfrey to wage war against the race of the Giants, as their fire was seen as a major threat that could possibly burn the Erdtree. Godfrey and his Crucible Knights, along with the forces of the Zamor Knights, eradicated the Giants, reduced the flame in the Great Forge to a mere ember of its former self. Queen Marika cursed the one remaining Fire Giant to tend the flame for eternity, leaving the Giant to endure eras of solitude. When fighting the Fire Giant, he will take his broken leg, sear it off himself, and offer it as a sacrifice for the Fell God of Fire, who grants him the power of divine flame to wield.
