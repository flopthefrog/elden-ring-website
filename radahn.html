Starscourge Radahn

Overview

During the eras of war and conflict, Radahn was easily one of the most feared and respected forces of the time. Born as a descendant of Rennala and Radagon, Radahn possessed the potential to become monumentally powerful. Similar to but not as desperately as Godrick, Radahn idolized the First Elden Lord Godfrey, modeling his armor after Godfrey’s symbol of the lion. Eventually, the bloodline of Radagon became intertwined with the Golden Lineage, promoting Radahn and his siblings Ranni and Rykard to the status of demigod.

The Starscourge

Radahn would go on to study and become one of the most powerful wielders of gravitational magic. Gravitational magic requires the user to not only have the acuity to cast such spells, but also an immense amount of strength in order to command effectively. Radahn took up his study in Sellia, the Town of Sorcery under an Alabaster Lord, an ancient race of beings that are said to be the kin of meteors who have an innate understanding of gravity. Radahn mastered gravitational magic to such a monumental level that, in order to save the town of Sellia, Radahn fought against the stars themselves, overpowering and suspending the stars, halting the cycle of the sky, delaying fate itself. This feat earned Radahn the title of Starscourge.

A Fall from Grace

Radahn went on to command an elite army of soldiers known as the Redmane Knights, who are rumored to have no weakness, and established the stronghold of Redmane Castle. Shortly after, a war broke out between the demigods, with the goal of ruling the Lands Between. After long periods of fighting and conflict, only Radahn and Malenia remained standing. It was during this fight that Malenaia released her Scarlet Rot, a ruthless plague that spread throughout the region of Caelid, infecting and decaying everything it touches. This Scarlet Rot deprecated Radahn from the inside, reducing his mind to that of a mindless beast. Radahn’s Redmane Knights dedicated themselves to containing the spread of the Scarlet Rot and putting Radahn out of his misery, organizing the Radahn festival to call on powerful warriors from across the land in order to give Radahn a worthy final battle.
