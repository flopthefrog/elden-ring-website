Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon

Overview

Rennala is a master astrologer who harnesses the power of the stars and the moon to conjure powerful sorceries. When she was young, Rennala gazed at the night sky longingly, chasing the stars and eventually discovering her true power within a source that many astrologers and scholars disregarded: the Full Moon. Rennala would go on to take over and become the headmaster of the Academy of Raya Lucaria, a school that specializes in the study of the stars and the magic drawn from them, called Glintblade Sorcery. Rennala also founded the house of Caria, an extremely powerful faction that would go on to possess immense magical power.

War and Peace

During Rennala’s time as headmaster, many wars were being fought across the lands, and her region of Liurnia of the Lakes was no exception to these conflicts. A champion and member of the Golden Order by the name of Radagon led Queen Marika’s army to conquer the region of Liurnia, encroaching on Rennala’s territory. Rennala took the full force of the academy, as well as her knights of Caria and pushed against the invading force. Two Liurnian Wars were fought, both resulting in stalemate, and eventually, Radagon resolved to end the war by swearing his love to Rennala, uniting Radagon’s house of the Erdtree and Rennala’s house of the Moon. Together, Radagon and Rennala had three children: Radahn, Rykard, and Ranni, all possessing Radagon’s red hair.
