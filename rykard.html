Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy

Overview

Rykard was one of three descendants of the Champion Radagon and the Full Moon Queen Rennala, and served under the Golden Order of Queen Marika. Initially, Rykard was a ruthless justicier for the Golden Order, with him and his organization of Inquisitors torturing and interrogating those considered to be threats and heretics to the order. Rykard managed this force within the Royal Capital of Leyndell, one of the most sacred and guarded areas within the Lands Between. Eventually, Rykard established the Volcano Manor, a house and base of operations for him and his company built upon Mt. Gelmir, not far from the capital. It is here that Rykard will come to discover long kept secrets hidden deep within the mountain. Rykard ventured into the depths of Mt. Gelmir, discovering a long abandoned religion from ages past that centered around the worshipping of a great and seemingly immortal serpent. Along with this, Rykard brought back knowledge of ancient magma hexes, of which he turned into newfound sorceries.

Lord of Blasphemy

When the war between the demigods broke out, Rykard did not participate in the fight for power; rather, Rykard decided to declare war on the Golden Order itself, aiming to debase the rule of the Erdtree of which he previously served under. This idea attracted many followers and strong warriors to swear their loyalty to Rykard and the Volcano Manor, building a formidable offensive force. The Golden Order’s attack on the Volcano Manor is often described as the most dishonorable and miserable battle of the entire war, as heinous acts of cannibalism and madness ravaged both forces, resulting in no clear victor. Rykard’s ambition became unchecked, and in order to achieve his blasphemous goal, Rykard ventured down into Mt. Gelmir, found the Great Serpent foretold in the ancient religion, and offered himself to it, letting the serpent consume him and his power whole. This act appalled many of Rykard’s followers, causing them to abandon him, with some even attempting and failing to slay the Lord of Blasphemy themselves. Rykard, now assimilated into the serpent, devoured his greatest warriors as well as his challengers, taking their power and making him a part of his “family”.
