The Lord of Ancients

Godrick the Grafted is a descendant of the Golden Lineage, a bloodline of demigods and powerful beings of divine origin. However, Godrick was born a disgrace, as he is merely a distant relative, not of direct offspring from Lord Godfrey and Queen Marika, the heads of the bloodline. Despite his tainted blood, however, Godrick fanatically idolizes his ancestor Godfrey, notably brandishing the crest of a lion, the symbol of Godfrey, on his greataxe.

Bayle's Betrayal

Godrick was shunned and chased out of his home of Leyndell, the Royal Capital. When Godrick fled, he took many treasures from the capital, including the Mimic Veil, an artifact that allows the user to take on a disguised form. It’s said that Godrick used this veil to camouflage himself as a woman in order to slip away undetected. Following his banishment, Godrick traveled South into the region of Limgrave, where he and other banished knights of Leyndell took over Stormveil Castle. Godrick would go on to fight and be utterly humiliated by the demigod Malenia, forced to beg and plead for his life. Following this crushing defeat, Godrick found himself determined to gain power and strength by any means necessary.

Suspended in Time

During this time, Godrick was considered meager and weak, especially compared to his relatives of his bloodline and his enemies. To compensate for his weakness, Godrick immersed himself in the grotesque and forbidden art of grafting, a most cowardly practice that involved melding limbs and parts of other beings to oneself in order to gain power. Godrick commanded his soldiers to search the lands for strong subjects of which he can graft to himself, which, in time, formed him into the being we see today. When fighting Godrick, in a desperate attempt to turn the tides, he will cut off his own arm in order to graft the head of a long deceased dragon to himself.
