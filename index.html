The Lore of Elden Ring- Homepage

About

This is a website dedicated to sharing the extremely in-depth lore of Elden Ring in a unique and interactive way. The world of Elden Ring is beyond immense, and with so many details to explore, this will be a very prolonged project. As of now, only basic lore of certain major characters/bosses have been recorded, but more will be coming soon! This also goes without saying but there are MAJOR SPOILERS for Elden Ring in the following content, so be aware.
